# Streamer & Influencer Program

**Owner:** Head of Brand / Influencer Manager
**Applies to:** Kick, Telegram, YouTube (restricted), Rumble. **Twitch: closed — see §1.**

---

## 1. Platform reality check

Read this before you build a media plan around the wrong platform.

| Platform | Status for a Curaçao casino | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Twitch** | **Closed** | Since Oct 2022 Twitch prohibits streaming slots, roulette and dice from sites *not licensed in the US or another jurisdiction that provides sufficient consumer protection*. Curaçao is not on that list. A creator streaming your slots gets suspended. Do not put a partner's channel at risk. |
| **Kick** | **Primary** | Kick permits gambling content and has a dedicated category. It exists in its current form largely because of this policy gap. Audience is younger, more LatAm-weighted, and lower CPM than Twitch. |
| **Telegram** | **Primary** | No platform gambling policy. Enormous in LatAm and among diaspora audiences. Channel owners behave like affiliates — treat them as a hybrid of §4 and the affiliate program. |
| **YouTube** | **Restricted** | Gambling content is age-gated and demonetised; links to unlicensed operators violate policy. Usable for *brand* content (interviews, lifestyle, sponsorship placement) — not for slot sessions. |
| **Rumble** | **Secondary** | Permissive, small, useful for creators already banned elsewhere. Low volume. |
| **X / Twitter** | **Support only** | Good for clips and giveaways, poor for acquisition. No paid gambling ads without local licence. |

**Budget implication:** ~65% Kick, ~30% Telegram, ~5% experimental. Anyone who pitches you a Twitch
plan for a Curaçao brand either does not know the policy or does not care what happens to the creator.

## 2. Sourcing and vetting

### 2.1 Where to find them

- Kick → Gambling category, sorted by viewers, filtered to your target-language streams.
- Telegram → search the market's slang for "cassino"/"tragamonedas"/"apostas"; look for channels with
  1k–50k members and *engagement*, not just member count.
- Competitor mentions — who is already promoting Stake, Roobet, Rollbit, BC.Game in your markets.
- Your own affiliate applicants — a good number of Telegram channel owners will apply as affiliates.

### 2.2 Vetting scorecard — score before you offer

| Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience geo match | 25% | ≥ 50% of audience in your **open** markets (not BR/CO) |
| Average concurrent viewers (30d) | 20% | Stable, not spiked. Check the floor, not the peak |
| Chat authenticity | 15% | Real conversation. Repeated generic phrases = bought chat |
| Existing casino deals | 15% | 1–2 is healthy. Five simultaneous = no audience trust left |
| Disclosure behaviour | 15% | **Does the creator currently disclose sponsorship and shadow balance?** If not, they are a liability |
| Payment/KYC readiness | 10% | Can invoice, can be paid, can pass your own KYC |

**Automatic disqualifiers:**
- Audience skews under 18 (check content, not stated demographics).
- History of undisclosed shadow-balance play.
- Promoting to Brazil or Colombia (you cannot accept the traffic; §5 of `01_affiliate_program.md`).
- Any content showing gambling as income, debt recovery, or a solution to money problems.

### 2.3 Verify viewership before you pay

Bought viewers are endemic. Cheap checks:
- Viewers-to-chatters ratio. Real streams run roughly 1 chatter per 15–40 viewers. 1:400 is bought.
- Viewer count at 03:00 local vs 21:00 local. Bots do not sleep.
- Ask for a screen-share of the creator's own analytics — geo, retention, returning viewers. A real
  partner shares it; a fake one negotiates around it.
- Run a **paid trial** (§3 Stage 3) before any long-term deal. Ever.

## 3. Onboarding funnel

| Stage | Duration | What happens | Gate to advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Contact** | Day 0 | Telegram DM, personalised, name the specific stream you watched | Reply |
| **2. Diligence** | Day 1–5 | Scorecard §2.2, analytics review, KYC, sanctions/PEP check | Score ≥ 70, KYC clear |
| **3. Paid trial** | Weeks 1–2 | 4 streams, shadow balance, unique code, CPA only — no retainer | ≥ 12 FTDs, cost/FTD ≤ 1.5× target |
| **4. Contract** | Week 3 | Full agreement §4, retainer + CPA + revshare | Signed, tax form on file |
| **5. Scale** | Month 2+ | Increase hours, add giveaways, co-branded content | Monthly KPI review §6 |
| **6. Exclusive** | Month 4+ | Category exclusivity, higher retainer, custom landing page | Top-quartile cost/FTD |

**Never skip Stage 3.** A creator who looks like 8,000 CCV and delivers 3 FTDs is common. The trial
costs you ~$1.5k and saves a $10k/month retainer mistake.

#### 4.3 Shadow (demo) balance — **the clause that matters most**

Operator will credit Creator's account with a non-withdrawable promotional balance ("**Shadow
Balance**") for on-stream play.

1. Shadow Balance is **Operator's money, not Creator's**. It cannot be withdrawn, transferred,
   converted, or used off-stream. Winnings from Shadow Balance play are not Creator's property.
2. **Creator must disclose Shadow Balance use.** On every stream funded by Shadow Balance, Creator
   will:
   - state verbally, within the first 5 minutes and at least once per hour thereafter, that the
     balance is provided by Operator and is not Creator's own money;
   - display a persistent on-screen overlay reading **"SPONSORED — BALANCE PROVIDED BY [BRAND] —
     NOT REAL PLAYER FUNDS — 18+"**, minimum 24px, continuously visible while gameplay is on screen;
   - include `#ad` or `#sponsored` in the stream title and in every clip derived from it.
3. Creator will **not** represent Shadow Balance play as personal risk, personal winnings, or a
   personal bankroll. Creator will not say or imply that they deposited their own funds when they
   did not.
4. Creator will not solicit, accept, or reference viewer donations, tips, or "bankroll help".
5. Breach of §4.3(2) or §4.3(3) is a **material breach**, terminates the agreement immediately, and
   forfeits all unpaid compensation accrued in the month of breach.

> **Why this clause is drafted this hard.** Undisclosed shadow-balance streaming is the single
> largest reputational and regulatory exposure in casino influencer marketing. It has produced
> platform-wide policy changes, regulator action, and the loss of licences. The disclosure is not a
> courtesy — it is the thing that keeps this channel legal and available.

#### 4.6 Content standards — Creator will not

- Claim, imply, or suggest that gambling is a source of income, a career, an investment, or a way to

- Suggest that gambling confers status, success, popularity, sexual success, or social superiority.
- Depict, target, or appeal to persons under 18, or use characters, music, or formats with primary

- Show or describe chasing losses, or present a loss as something to be won back.
- Present any outcome as guaranteed, due, "hot", or predictable.
- Stream while intoxicated, or depict gambling alongside alcohol or drug use.
- Disclose or promote Operator to users in a Prohibited Territory (Schedule C).
- Use Operator's brand assets in any way not in the brand kit, or alter licence/RG marks.

- Every sponsored stream carries the 18+ mark and a visible link to Operator's responsible-gambling

  deposit limits and self-exclusion are in your account settings."*
- Creator will not promote to, or knowingly engage with, any viewer who identifies as
  self-excluded or under 18, and will ban such users from chat.
- Creator will complete Operator's RG briefing before the first sponsored stream.

## 5. Gamified giveaway formats

All formats below assume the RG constraints in §6. Each is designed so the **viewer never has to
deposit to participate** — that is what keeps them promotable and keeps them out of "lottery"
classification in most jurisdictions. Take local advice on prize promotion law before running any of
them at scale.

### 5.1 Bonus Hunt / Bonus Buy Opening
The genre staple. Creator collects N bonus rounds across a session, then opens them all at the end.

- **Format:** Creator buys/collects 20 bonuses over ~90 minutes, then opens sequentially. Viewers
  guess the total return before opening starts.
- **Viewer hook:** predict-the-outcome, no deposit required to guess.
- **Prize:** $500 split across the 5 closest guesses; entry by chat command.
- **Why it works:** it front-loads 90 minutes of watch time before the payoff.
- **Constraint:** Shadow Balance disclosure per §4.3 applies to the entire session.

### 5.2 Wager Race (leaderboard)
- **Format:** 7-day leaderboard on total *wagered* using the creator's code. Top 20 share a prize
  pool.
- **Pool:** $3,000 → 30/18/12/8/6 then 5×$100, 10×$46.
- **Why wagered, not deposited:** a deposit-based leaderboard directly incentivises depositing more
  than intended and is the format most likely to be flagged. Wager-based races reward play the
  player was already doing.
- **Hard constraints:** exclude anyone with an active deposit limit, cooling-off, or self-exclusion.
  Publish the leaderboard live so nobody chases an invisible position. Cap individual prizes so the
  top spot is not worth a spending spiral.

### 5.3 Code-Drop Raffle
- **Format:** Creator drops a code in chat at random intervals. Viewers redeem on-site for a small
  free-spin package. No deposit required.
- **Prize:** 20 free spins on a $0.20 stake, $50 max win, 1× wagering.
- **Why it works:** it is the cheapest possible registration driver and it converts *viewers* into
  *accounts* without a deposit ask. The deposit ask comes later, from CRM.
- **Watch:** cap redemptions per IP/device or you are funding a bonus-abuse ring.

### 5.4 Community Slot Battle
- **Format:** Creator vs. a rival creator, or creator vs. chat's pick. Two slots, equal balance,
  highest multiplier in 50 spins wins.
- **Viewer hook:** team allegiance; viewers pick a side by chat command and the winning side splits
  a pool.
- **Cross-promo value:** two audiences, one production cost.

### 5.5 "Beat the Streamer"
- **Format:** Creator posts a target multiplier from their session. Any player who beats it on the
  same game that week wins a fixed prize.
- **Why it works:** it converts passive viewers into players with a concrete, non-monetary goal.
- **Constraint:** fixed prize, not a jackpot. Never scale the prize with the player's stake — that is
  a stake-escalation mechanic.

### 5.6 Milestone Unlock (non-gambling)
- **Format:** community-wide goal ("2,000 followers this month") unlocks a prize drop for everyone
  who has an account, regardless of play.
- **Why it is here:** it is the only format on this list that rewards audience growth rather than
  gambling activity, and it should be roughly a third of your giveaway calendar. It gives the
  creator something to promote in weeks when you do not want to push play volume.

### 5.7 Formats to refuse

| Format | Why not |
|---|---|
| Deposit-matched leaderboard | Directly incentivises deposit escalation |
| "Loss-back" / rebate race | Pays for losing — pushes loss-chasing |
| Countdown-to-claim drops | Manufactures urgency to deposit *now* |
| Daily-streak rewards that reset | A punishment mechanic dressed as a game |
| Prize scaling with stake size | Rewards the biggest bettor for being the biggest bettor |
| Anything targeting a named viewer's losses | Unacceptable, and the clip will outlive the campaign |

## 6. Responsible gambling controls in the influencer channel

These are enforced by you, not by the creator's goodwill.

1. **Suppression list.** Any player with an active deposit limit, loss limit, cooling-off, or
   self-exclusion is excluded from every leaderboard, race, and giveaway. Enforced in the CRM
   segment, not by manual review. See `../crm_retention/automation_flows.json` → `rg_suppression`.
2. **Overlay assets are provided by you**, pre-built with the 18+ mark, RG link, and disclosure line
   baked in. Do not let creators build their own — they will make the mark smaller.
3. **Monthly content audit.** Pull 3 random hours of each partner's sponsored footage per month.
   Check disclosure frequency, overlay presence, and §4.6 breaches. Score it; keep the record.
4. **Clip liability.** Clips outlive streams. Require `#ad` in clip titles and audit the creator's
   clip channel, not just the live stream.
5. **Escalation path.** First §4.6 breach: written warning, 48h to remove content. Second:
   retainer suspended for the month. Third: termination.
